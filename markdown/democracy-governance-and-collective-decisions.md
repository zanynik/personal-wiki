# Democracy, Governance, and Collective Decisions

**Core idea:** Ideas about political systems, voting, online coordination, public services, and how societies make decisions.

## Working Definition

Governance is treated as a coordination problem: how can large groups sense needs, allocate attention, and make decisions without letting money, population pressure, or party machinery distort the signal?

## Main Threads

- Formal voting is questioned when economic power shapes what choices are visible.
- Online systems are imagined as possible tools for more direct, contextual decision-making.
- Public services and population pressure appear as concrete stress tests for governance.
- Political parties are treated as design objects, not sacred defaults.

## Guiding Questions

- How can collective decisions represent real needs rather than money or media power?
- What would online democratic systems make possible?
- How should public services respond to population, demand, and inequality?
