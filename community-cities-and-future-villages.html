Community, Cities, and Future Villages

Core idea: The recurring imagination of community after alienation: cities, villages, strangers, travel, and future social forms.

Working Definition

The future social dream combines the warmth and mutual recognition of village life with the openness, diversity, and cultural mixing of modern cities.

Main Threads

  • Modern cities create anonymity and cultural richness at the same time.
  • Older village life represents recognition and belonging, but also possible gossip, constraint, and closedness.
  • The desired future synthesizes openness and intimacy into international villages.
  • Travel/location notes often become prompts for imagining different social architectures.

Guiding Questions

  • Can modern diversity and older village intimacy be combined?
  • How can urban life become less anonymous without becoming closed or oppressive?
  • What would an international village feel like?
