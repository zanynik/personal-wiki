# AI and Human Flourishing

**Core idea:** How AI should extend human agency, learning, creativity, and emotional safety rather than merely automate labor.

## Working Definition

AI is treated as a civilizational instrument: not just a productivity machine, but a medium for coaching, mediation, creativity, self-understanding, and collective coordination.

## Main Threads

- AI as a companion for thought: helping clarify, remember, connect, and gently challenge ideas.
- AI as social mediator: a neutral party that can reduce misunderstanding if designed with care.
- AI as economic shock: automation, job loss, abundance of intelligence, and the need for new institutions.
- AI as creative amplifier: writing, coding, music, games, film, and personal knowledge systems.

## Guiding Questions

- What kind of AI makes people more alive rather than more replaceable?
- How can AI mediate, teach, coach, and coordinate without becoming a new authority structure?
- What happens when intelligence becomes cheap, abundant, and conversational?
