Learning, Intelligence, and Creativity

Core idea: Ideas about curiosity, reading, music, games, movies, reinforcement learning, and intelligence as a lived process.

Working Definition

Learning is not merely accumulation. It is the emergence of love, attention, play, and repeated contact with a domain until intelligence becomes embodied.

Main Threads

  • Deep learning begins when time, freedom, and affection let a person fall in love with something.
  • Creativity often appears as translation: idea to film, game, podcast, tool, model, or social structure.
  • Reinforcement learning and human learning are used as analogies for motivation and feedback.
  • Music, reading, and games become examples of playful intelligence rather than side interests.

Guiding Questions

  • How does love for a subject emerge?
  • What is the role of play in intelligence?
  • How can ideas become games, films, tools, or practices?
