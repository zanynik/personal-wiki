Media, Social Networks, and Information Flow

Core idea: Notes on feeds, NOSTR, social media, podcasts, knowledge circulation, and AI-mediated information environments.

Working Definition

Information systems are judged by whether they increase agency, context, trust, and thoughtful connection rather than by engagement or virality.

Main Threads

  • Feeds should reflect intention and context, not only platform incentives.
  • NOSTR and folder-based feeds point toward user-owned information flow.
  • Podcasts and social media appear as both inspiration sources and design problems.
  • A personal wiki is itself an answer: a slower, more navigable information ecology.

Guiding Questions

  • What would healthier information flow look like?
  • Can feeds be built from personal folders and intentions rather than engagement traps?
  • What social protocol supports independence and cooperation?
