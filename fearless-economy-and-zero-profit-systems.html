Fearless Economy and Zero-Profit Systems

Core idea: A social and economic design space centered on reducing fear, coercion, extraction, and status pressure.

Working Definition

An economy is evaluated by how much fear it removes from ordinary life, how much coercion it avoids, and whether it lets people contribute without being trapped by scarcity games.

Main Threads

  • Fear is treated as a hidden operating system behind work, status, money, and compliance.
  • Profit is questioned as a default coordinating principle, especially when it intensifies insecurity.
  • Zero-profit and Vipassana-like institutional models appear as experiments in non-extractive organization.
  • Economic design is linked to psychological safety and the ability to act from interest rather than desperation.

Guiding Questions

  • What economic structures remove fear as a behavioral engine?
  • How can useful work be organized without profit-maximization as the central metric?
  • What would make basic security compatible with autonomy and creativity?
