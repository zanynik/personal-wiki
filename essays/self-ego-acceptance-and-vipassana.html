Self, Ego, Acceptance, and Vipassana

These notes treat inner life as practical work. The point is not self-description for its own sake, but the slow training of attention, acceptance, and conduct.

Awareness As A Daily Practice

Daily practice involves continuing meditation to deepen understanding of reality. Key attitudes include welcoming, accepting, letting go, respecting, loving, and kindness. During meditation, one can observe bodily sensations—such as shaking—as mere experiences, not as the self, recognizing their impermanent nature. This practice helps in accepting the flow of life, where things come and go. Reflecting on diverse experiences, like those of less privileged individuals, can offer different perspectives on awareness and resilience.
