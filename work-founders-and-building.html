Work, Founders, and Building

Core idea: A practical strand about founders, office reflections, work, products, usefulness, and building with AI.

Working Definition

Building is a practical expression of philosophy: products, companies, and workflows become tests of whether an idea can reduce fear, increase agency, or make intelligence usable.

Main Threads

  • Founder thinking is tied to mission, usefulness, and the emotional reality of building.
  • Work is questioned when it becomes status theater or fear compliance.
  • AI changes the cost of execution, making taste, purpose, and judgment more central.
  • Products are evaluated by whether they change real behavior or merely look impressive.

Guiding Questions

  • What is worth building?
  • What makes work meaningful rather than merely performative?
  • How should AI change the founder/product loop?
