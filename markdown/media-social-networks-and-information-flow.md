# Media, Social Networks, and Information Flow

The media notes are about attention sovereignty. Feeds, social networks, podcasts, NOSTR, folders, and personal knowledge systems all appear as ways information moves through a life.

## Core Thesis

Information systems should increase agency, context, memory, and genuine connection. Most modern feeds optimize engagement. A healthier feed would be built from intentions, relationships, projects, and personal knowledge rather than platform incentives.

## Folder-Based Feeds

One recurring idea is that feeds could come from folders. Instead of a platform deciding what appears, a person's own knowledge base, saved sources, projects, and trusted people could generate a slower, more meaningful stream.

This wiki is an example: raw notes become organized pages that can be browsed without algorithmic pressure.

## NOSTR And Protocol Thinking

NOSTR appears as a symbol of protocol-based social media: identity and communication not fully owned by a platform. The deeper interest is independence. A good information network should let people move, own their data, and coordinate without being trapped.

## AI As Information Companion

AI can help summarize, route, connect, and contextualize information. But it can also become another filter bubble. The desirable AI companion should help the user remember their own values and long-term questions, not just serve novelty.

## Open Questions

- What should a personal feed optimize for?
- How can information systems become calmer and more intentional?
- What belongs in a public social network versus a private second brain?
- Can AI help connect ideas without hijacking attention?
