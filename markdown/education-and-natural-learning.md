# Education and Natural Learning

**Core idea:** A critique of standardized education and an alternate vision where learning follows need, curiosity, context, and love.

## Working Definition

Education is treated as an environment-design problem: create conditions where children and adults discover needs, interests, service, and practice rather than being pushed through abstract compliance.

## Main Threads

- The current education system is linked to economic incentives, comparison, and abstract curriculum.
- Natural learning begins with need, interest, play, observation, and repeated contact.
- Children need freedom and time to fall in love with domains before being measured against them.
- Education reform is treated as part of wider economic and social-system reform.

## Guiding Questions

- What would education look like if it followed curiosity instead of compliance?
- How do children discover love for a subject?
- What should be taught directly, and what should emerge from environment and practice?
